# fsd-predictive-behaviour
# goal: develop an AI model that can identify dangerous driving behavior and make rational moves to minimize the risk or accidents from scratch
# why: i noticed that Tesla is great at making super fast decisions when it comes to emergency situations, for example the car in front of us slam a hard brake or suddenly another car cuts in and ego car has to maneuver through the danger. However, humans tend to perform better at identifying weird driving behavaior during the early stage and usually change lane + overtake in order to stay safe. And since my girlfriend is a safety first girl while I'm a convenience first boy, this project is a way for me to get one step closer to my dream to get in the car and start sleeping and keep my beloved one safe.
